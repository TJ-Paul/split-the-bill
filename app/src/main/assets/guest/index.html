❓ How does this work?
  1. Type your name and what you ordered with its price from the menu.
  2. Tap Add me to the bill. The person who shared this page sees it on their phone straight away.
  3. Need to change something? Tap Edit on your order any time.

Handy tricks

  • Prices can be sums: 120+80, 250×2. Use the + − × ÷ keys under the price box.
  • Shared a dish? Type the total divided by the people: 900÷3.
  • Had the same as a friend? Tap Copy order on theirs, then change what's different.
  • You can only change orders added from this phone. The host can change everything.
